Company focus

Underwater ROV developer and SubNav software provider; standard vehicles, named specialist configurations and surface/accessory roles distinguished

  • SubNav OS is Oceanbotics in-house ROV control software
  • ROV control and customization functions are tied to separately named vehicles; software is not another underwater robot
  • Current navigation identifies standard SRV-8/SRV-8X, compact SRV-8C and ATS/MDV specialist configurations. SRV-8 first customer shipment is explicitly May 13, 2019; company history launches SRV-8X Optimus March 22, 2023. Engineering/founding, special-division launch and PDF revision/upload dates are not silently treated as every variant’s release.
  • Standard SRV-8 listed 18.6 kg versus 13.2 kg without batteries and SRV-8X 32 kg versus 24 kg without are corroborated by HTML columns and image-sheet OCR. Standard up-to-eight-hour/two-module operation differs from compact four-hour/one-module and linked ATS four-to-six-hour specifications. Direct surface power and station/software roles are separate.
  • SRV-8C depth text conflicts: page305m/1640ft versus OCR sheet350m/1148ft and September 2026 guide350m. Standard SRV-8/SRV-8X tether strengths are also reversed between HTML copper 544 kg/fiber 350 kg and sheet OCR copper 350 kg/fiber 544 kg. Configuration/revision/typo bases remain unresolved; strengths are not cargo ratings.
  • Three image-only PDFs required isolated local OCR with text, confidence and word rectangles; crop checks corroborate selected numbers. Compact total-weight unit reads 13 kg at 300 dpi versus13ks in alternate 450 dpi crop and remains explicitly qualified. No visual PDF/image/video/CAD verification is claimed.
  • ATS is a named acoustic recovery configuration with its own linked historical specifications, not automatically current standard hardware. MDV is explicitly standard ROV hardware with specialist clearance accessories; generic identity/role only, no accessory operating details or transferred configured mass/depth/runtime. SubNav/SubNav-X, TPS, surface stations, USBL/DVL, sonar and external manipulator suppliers are separate roles.
  • Quotes, stock, independent software/security/safety performance, exact cargo/charge/speed/positioning values and exclusive/leading claims remain unverified. Public manufacturer positioning is not proof of an independently unique USP or market/search leadership.
