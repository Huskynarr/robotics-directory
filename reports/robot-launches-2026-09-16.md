# Robot announcements and launches: 18 August–16 September 2026

Research cutoff: 2026-09-16 (Europe/Berlin). Window: 30 calendar dates, inclusive.

Broad review across the eight catalog categories, including IFA, WRC and IMTS news,
manufacturer newsrooms and manufacturer-issued releases. This is a sourced discovery
pass, not a claim to capture every robot announced worldwide. Specs describe manufacturer
claims, not independent test results. Only named products or explicitly named series were
added; accessories, software-only releases, funding-only news and generic show appearances
were excluded. Existing work in the catalog was preserved.

Announcement, preorder, prototype demonstration, regional debut and commercial release
are distinguished in each description. Future dates are plans as of the cutoff. The CSV's
`releaseDate` field is labelled “Released” on the site, so it remains blank for announcements
and scheduled launches without verified release evidence. Prices retain their source market
and currency. Unknown specs and unlicensed images remain blank; the site uses its existing
image fallback. Existing OlloNi and An'An page IDs are retained.

36 new catalog entries (including named series), 3 existing entries updated; validated working-tree catalog: 1,255 products (including 10 pre-existing uncommitted entries, excluded from this publication).
